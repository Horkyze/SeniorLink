# SeniorLink 0.1.10 — interactive heart-rate charts

Explore saved heart-rate readings with touch controls in the expanded charts:

- **Pinch to zoom** down to a one-minute window.
- **Move two fingers** to browse earlier or later times.
- **Tap or drag one finger** to move a dotted marker between readings. The label
  shows the saved BPM and full recorded date and time, including seconds.
- Tap **Reset chart** to restore the full time range.

Tap the dashboard's heart-rate card or wearable group for the selected day's
chart, or open **Wearable** for the one-hour or 24-hour view. Vertical swipes still
scroll the page. Accessibility actions let you zoom, move through time and step
between readings. Changing the phone, day or preset range resets the chart view.

The marker snaps to actual saved readings. Missing readings remain missing, and
the graph still represents saved pulse summaries, not an ECG or a live measurement.

## Download and update

Download **SeniorLink-0.1.10-debug.apk** from this release's assets. **Install as an
update without uninstalling** to preserve identity, pairing, settings and history.
The APK uses the same signing certificate as the published 0.1.9 APK. Sync protocol
3 is unchanged: versions 0.1.7–0.1.10 can communicate; paired phones on 0.1.6 or
older must also be updated.

This is a debug-signed family pilot prerelease for Android 8.0 or newer, including
ARM64, ARMv7 and x86-64. Both roles use the same APK.

## Verification

- All 40 core and 69 Android JVM tests pass. Build and lint pass with zero errors.
- Eight final-APK emulator tests cover chart gestures, health overview and the
  daily-summary workflow, including scrubbing inside the preview sheet.
- The source-build gesture tests also pass at 2× text size. Synthetic chart and
  sheet screenshots were visually reviewed.
- APK signature and signing compatibility with 0.1.9 verify. Native libraries
  remain unchanged, and ABI and 16 KB alignment checks pass. Archive parts
  reconstruct the exact tested APK.

Physical-phone touch behavior and TalkBack still need device checks. This update
makes no new physical wearable compatibility or medical-reliability claims.
