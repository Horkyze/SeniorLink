# SeniorLink 0.1.7 — Calm design, heart-rate graph and batteries

See recorded heart-rate trends and the battery remaining on your family member's
phone and compatible smartwatch in the new Calm dashboard.

## What changed

- A soft green interface with a pink heart-rate chart, separate phone and watch
  battery cards, and bottom navigation for Updates, Location, Wearable, Phones and
  Settings. Large text stacks the battery cards and wraps navigation into two rows.
- **1 hour / 24 hours** heart-rate history, based on the last pulse in each saved
  two-minute summary. The graph breaks across missing readings and reported loss
  of sensor contact. Values keep their own recording times; this is not an ECG.
- Phone battery percentage and available charging state, shared only after
  explicitly enabling **Settings → Share phone battery**. Pause sharing before
  changing the setting, save, then start sharing. Sampling occurs at startup and
  approximately every five minutes while sharing is active.
- Separate smartwatch battery percentage where the wearable exposes standard
  Bluetooth battery data. Low battery is labelled at 20% or below. Missing readings
  show **Unknown**; old readings are marked rather than presented as current.
- Family selection stays consistent between Updates and Wearable. Readings stay
  scoped to the approved family phone and its selected watch.

Phone-battery sharing is off by default. Caregiver phones do not collect their own
data. Pause and feature withdrawal retain their existing behavior.

## Download and update

Install **SeniorLink-0.1.7-debug.apk** on **every paired phone**. This release uses
sync protocol 3 and cannot synchronize with 0.1.6 or older, including existing
check-ins and wearable updates.

The APK uses the same signing certificate as the published 0.1.6 APK and earlier
pilot releases. **Install it as an update without uninstalling** to keep identity,
pairing, settings and history. Use **Settings → App updates → Check for updates**
or download the APK from this release's assets.

This remains a debug-signed family pilot prerelease for Android 8.0 or later,
including ARM64, ARMv7 and x86-64. Both roles use the same APK.

## Verification and device limits

- All 40 core and 48 Android JVM tests pass. Build and lint pass with zero errors.
- Dashboard, wearable, consent/service and native encrypted delivery checks pass
  on the Android 17 / API 37.1 ARM64 emulator. The final Calm interface passes
  13 selected UI/workflow checks and four dashboard checks at 2× text size.
- APK signing compatibility, native ABI/16 KB alignment and reconstruction from
  the six archive parts are verified.

**Physical Galaxy Fit3 compatibility is still unverified.** A watch that does not
expose standard Bluetooth battery data cannot provide its battery level through
this integration. Real-phone background reliability also needs device testing.
SeniorLink does not provide medical interpretations or emergency alerts.

See the [wearable setup and device checklist](https://github.com/Horkyze/SeniorLink/blob/v0.1.7/docs/wearables.md)
and [verification record](https://github.com/Horkyze/SeniorLink/blob/v0.1.7/docs/verification.md).

Download `SHA256SUMS` alongside the APK to verify it:

```sh
# macOS
shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS
# Linux
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
```
