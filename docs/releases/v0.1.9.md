# SeniorLink 0.1.9 — background updates and daily summary

Sharing and background receiving now start automatically after an approved phone
connection. The app requests recovery after ordinary process interruptions,
reboot and app replacement, and reconnects when Android permits network work.
Small toggles in **Settings → Background updates** let you pause sharing or
background receiving. A saved pause remains in effect after reconnecting,
restarting or adding another phone.

Existing paired installations without a saved background preference adopt the
default when opened. Android permissions and individual collection choices still
apply. Caregiver mode receives updates without collecting its own phone's data.
A notification remains visible while background work is active.

## Daily summary dashboard

The Updates page now shows compact heart-rate and battery cards, followed by
daily groups for check-ins, wearable readings, locations and phone activity.
Messages appear when records are available. Tap a group to preview its saved
updates, then open full history with date/type filters and **Load earlier**.
Counts include all retained records for the selected phone and day. Large text
stacks the cards and keeps the controls accessible.

## Background availability and battery

Settings shows Android's battery-optimization status and offers its optional
background-operation exemption dialog. Caregivers check roughly every 15 seconds
while open and every minute in the background when execution is allowed. Existing
collection intervals are unchanged, and the app uses no continuous CPU wake lock.

Android can still delay or stop execution. Force stop requires reopening the app;
observed system user stops suppress automatic recovery until reopening. Doze or
offline periods delay delivery until both phones are reachable together. Fresh
background location recovery needs the optional **Allow all the time** permission;
otherwise other enabled features recover and location waits for visible reopening.

Physical-device battery drain and overnight reliability remain device checks.
Phone-battery sharing reports the whole device's charge, not SeniorLink's own
energy consumption.

## Download and update

Download **SeniorLink-0.1.9-debug.apk** from this release's assets. **Install as an
update without uninstalling** to preserve identity, pairing, settings and history.
The APK uses the same signing certificate as the published 0.1.8 APK. Sync protocol
3 is unchanged: 0.1.7, 0.1.8 and 0.1.9 can communicate; paired phones on 0.1.6 or
older must also be updated.

This is a debug-signed family pilot prerelease for Android 8.0 or newer, including
ARM64, ARMv7 and x86-64. Both roles use the same APK. It does not guarantee
continuous monitoring or provide medical or emergency alerts.
