# SeniorLink 0.1.12 — heart-rate fix and battery history

The dashboard heart-rate graph now includes the latest received pulse before its
two-minute summary is saved. Its time window also refreshes as readings arrive,
so a new reading no longer sits beyond an out-of-date chart clock. Old readings
keep their original timestamps and stay outside the last-hour view when appropriate.

## Phone battery timeline

- Phone battery now uses the same chart as heart rate, with a 0–100% scale.
- The dashboard shows the last hour. Tap **Phone battery** for the recorded day's
  interactive chart. **Wearable** also offers one-hour and 24-hour battery views.
- Pinch to zoom, move two fingers to browse time, and tap or drag to inspect a
  recorded percentage, exact timestamp and charging status.
- The latest **Charging**, **Not charging** or **Charging state unavailable** status
  appears on the card. It describes the recorded time, not a live connection to
  the charger. Collection remains approximately every five minutes while enabled
  and sharing. Gaps longer than ten minutes are left disconnected in the chart.
- Missing readings stay missing. Each family's battery history remains separate;
  caregiver mode does not collect the caregiver phone's battery.

## Download and update

Download **SeniorLink-0.1.12-debug.apk** from this release's assets and install it
as an update without uninstalling to preserve identity, pairing, settings and
history. Its signing certificate matches published 0.1.11.

Protocol 3 is unchanged. Versions 0.1.7–0.1.12 can sync together; no coordinated
upgrade is required for these chart changes. Update the viewing phone for the new
charts. This remains a debug-signed family pilot prerelease for Android 8.0 or
newer, including ARM64, ARMv7 and x86-64.
