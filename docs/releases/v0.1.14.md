# SeniorLink 0.1.14 — less background battery use and a consistent look

SeniorLink now waits for an actual change instead of checking every 15 seconds,
and its screens share one visual style. No new information is collected, and
consent, caregiver approval and Pause work as before.

## Less background work

- **Mailbox uploads run when there is something to send.** The sharing phone
  uploads when a new update is saved or consent changes, retries unsent work
  after 15 seconds, and otherwise checks caregiver receipts about every five
  minutes. Previously it contacted the mailbox every 15 seconds.
- **Pause, feature withdrawal and caregiver removal still reach the mailbox at
  once** while the phone is online, and after reconnecting when it is offline.
- **Caregiver phones check about every five minutes while the app is closed**,
  instead of every minute. Opening the app checks at once. While the app is open
  it still checks about every 15 seconds.
- **Telegram forwarding** sends when a new update is saved and retries failed
  posts; it no longer polls every 15 seconds.
- **Wearables:** after connecting, the app asks Android for a low-power Bluetooth
  link. An out-of-range band is retried at most every five minutes instead of
  every minute, so reconnecting after returning can take up to five minutes.
- A hidden app no longer reloads its history after every background update.

Collection is unchanged: heart-rate readings are still received continuously and
summarized every two minutes, phone battery is sampled about every five minutes
and location about every 15 minutes.

## Consistent look

- Every card uses the same white surface, outline, corner radius and spacing.
- One type scale is used across screens, and the top bar matches the navigation bar.
- Each switch in Settings carries its explanation, which screen readers announce
  with the switch. Fine print is visually secondary.
- Day navigation and list rows use arrow icons with spoken labels.
- **App updates** and **Encrypted queued delivery** use the same panel as other
  settings. No wording was changed.

## Download and update

Download **SeniorLink-0.1.14-debug.apk** from the release assets. Install it as an
update without uninstalling to keep identity, pairing, settings and history.
Its signing certificate matches published 0.1.13. The local database schema is
unchanged.

Direct sync remains protocol 3 and works with versions 0.1.7–0.1.14. The mailbox
protocols are unchanged, so 0.1.13 and 0.1.14 phones can be mixed. This remains a
debug-signed family pilot prerelease for Android 8.0 or newer, including ARM64,
ARMv7 and x86-64.

## Verification

- 46 core tests and 97 Android JVM tests pass. Android build, lint and native
  ABI/16 KB alignment checks pass.
- On the Android 17 ARM64 emulator, 45 of 49 instrumentation tests pass and four
  are skipped because they need fixture arguments. Two of the 45 passed only when
  run alone, for reasons of test environment.
- In-place upgrade from 0.1.13 succeeds on the emulator. All native libraries are
  unchanged; seven archive parts reconstruct the tested APK exactly.
- **Battery use has not been measured.** The savings follow from fewer scheduled
  wakeups and requests; no emulator or phone measurement was made. Mailbox and
  Telegram timing with live services, the low-power Bluetooth link on a physical
  wearable, and a visual review on real phones remain device acceptance checks.

See the [verification record](https://github.com/Horkyze/SeniorLink/blob/v0.1.14/docs/verification.md)
for performed checks and remaining limits. Download `SHA256SUMS` alongside the APK:

```sh
# macOS
shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS
# Linux
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
```
