# SeniorLink 0.1.6 — direct Bluetooth wearables

Connect a Bluetooth LE wearable directly to the sharing phone and send its
readings to approved caregivers through encrypted iroh synchronization. Samsung
Health and Health Connect are not required for this collection path.

## What changed

- Choose a nearby or previously paired wearable under **Settings → Bluetooth
  wearable**, enable **Share wearable readings**, save, then start sharing.
  Android requests the Bluetooth permissions needed for scanning and connecting.
- Collect standard heart rate, skin contact, optional beat intervals and energy,
  battery, temperature, pulse oximeter and blood-pressure measurements where the
  device exposes them, plus device information. Invalid readings are withheld.
- Open **Wearable** on either phone for current or cached readings, their receipt
  times and retained summaries. The sharing phone also shows connection status,
  available capabilities and discovered Bluetooth service IDs.
- Save the first health reading promptly and aggregate subsequent readings into
  approximately two-minute summaries. Each caregiver catches up independently;
  optional existing Telegram forwarding includes wearable summaries.
- Reconnect while sharing is active. Pause stops collection; disabling wearable
  sharing removes its retained events and queued Telegram messages on the sharing
  phone. Previously delivered caregiver history cannot be recalled.

## Galaxy Fit3 and compatibility

**Physical Galaxy Fit3 testing is still outstanding.** Community observations
suggest heart rate and skin contact are available through standard Bluetooth,
but this release has been verified using simulated peripherals and an Android
emulator. Standard battery data may not be exposed by the Fit3.

Enable continuous heart-rate measurement on the band before testing. Proprietary
sleep, steps, stress, ECG and stored history are not collected. Measurements missed
while Bluetooth is disconnected cannot be recovered later. Other devices can
provide additional supported standard measurements. This integration does not
replace initial band setup or firmware management.

Follow the [setup and real-device checklist](https://github.com/Horkyze/SeniorLink/blob/v0.1.6/docs/wearables.md).

## Download and update

Download **SeniorLink-0.1.6-debug.apk** from the assets and install it on **every
paired phone**. Sync protocol 2 cannot communicate with older SeniorLink versions,
including for existing phone updates.

The APK uses the same signing certificate as the published 0.1.0–0.1.5 APKs.
**Do not uninstall first** if you want to keep identity, pairing, settings and
history. Versions 0.1.3–0.1.5 check for this update when opened; older versions
can use the APK download directly.

This is a debug-signed family pilot prerelease for Android 8.0 or later, including
ARM64, ARMv7 and x86-64. Both roles use the same APK. It does not provide medical
interpretations or emergency alerts.
